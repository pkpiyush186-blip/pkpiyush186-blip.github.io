The big idea

Don't review the dish. Tell the tension behind it.

On my channel, stories with a clear tension far outperformed plain labels. Restaurants are full of those stories: a family recipe, a risky opening, a chef who refused to change one dish.

Pillar 01

Discover

Useful, saveable city guides that answer "where should we eat tonight?"

Pillar 02

Backstory

Short human stories about chefs, owners and places worth protecting.

Pillar 03

Community

Polls, debates and local picks that make followers part of the food culture.
